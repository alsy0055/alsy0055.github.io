How I'd use it
  • Start at level 1 with the first 30. Move up a level once you score about 9 out of 10 twice in a row.
  • Then widen the words: first 70, then all 118, then the core words.
  • Anything you miss comes back a few questions later. "My weak ones" collects what you keep getting wrong.
  • Say every answer out loud when it plays. That's free speaking practice.

Keys: 1–4 to answer, Enter for next, R to play the sound again.
