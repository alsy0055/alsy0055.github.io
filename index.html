Checking for a Chinese voice…

Start at the top and work down. Tap any tile, word or the ▶ to hear it. The core words stay on the right so you can look a word up while you read. In example sentences each word shows pinyin, character, then its meaning. Colors: 1st 2nd 3rd 4th neutral. Pinyin is written the standard way, so 不 and 一 already show their changed tone. A dotted underline marks a 3rd tone usually said as 2nd before another 3rd (a rough guide).
