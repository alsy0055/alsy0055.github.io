Roadmap to HSK 4

A rough guide. Timing depends on daily hours. The 2026 HSK standard puts HSK 4 at about 2,000 words, more than the old version.

Weeks 1–3

Pinyin and tones

  • Tones and Sounds sections above, until you can say each tile without looking.
  • Shadowing: play a phrase, pause, repeat it with the same melody.
Months 1–3

Survival Mandarin (HSK 1 level)

  • Greetings, introduction, survival phrases and core words on this page.
  • Basic patterns: 是 sentences, 吗 questions, 不 / 没 negation, the measure word 个, time before the verb (我明天去).
  • Start an HSK 1 deck in Anki: about 15 minutes a day.
Months 3–9

HSK 2–3: real input

  • A textbook series mapped to HSK (ask your friend which edition learners use now).
  • Graded readers, and video with Chinese + English subtitles together (the Language Reactor extension does this on YouTube and Netflix). Peppa Pig in Mandarin, 小猪佩奇, is a classic beginner show.
  • Speak every week: your friend and your Discord servers. Awkward is fine.
Months 9–24

HSK 4 and workplace Mandarin

  • HSK 4 vocabulary, mock tests, then the exam.
  • Work vocabulary for GA and operations: meetings, schedules, repairs, vendors.
  • Add "Mandarin (HSK level)" to your CV and LinkedIn as soon as you pass any level.

Daily routine (40 minutes)

15 min

Anki flashcards. New words plus reviews. Don't skip a day; reviews pile up.

15 min

Listening. This page, a lesson, or a show with dual subtitles.

10 min

Speaking. Shadow phrases aloud, or message your friend in Mandarin.

Tools
